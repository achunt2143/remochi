# Remochi v0.6.0 — 2026-11-24

This release brings remochi in line with the Mochi / webOS design guide and the Pyxis identity: a lighter pane, signature corners (the scallop on edge-anchored panes, the waterdrop on floating surfaces), a type ladder with a `Label` and a display `Header`, colour jobs with contrast floors and greyscale cues, three motion modes (Full, Reduced, Off) plus high-contrast and reduced-transparency modes, and peeks and width classes on `RepanelStack`. It also carries the work from the fix branch that 0.5.1 never shipped: nubbins, divider caps and the grabber are drawn in code, and the `Spinner` is an SVG. The PNG and GIF sprites are gone from `src`.

**This release has visible and behaviour changes — see [Upgrading from 0.5.x](#upgrading-from-05x) before bumping.**

## Highlights

- **A lighter Mochi pane.** Panes draw a 1px left edge in the new `--mochi-pane-edge` (`--mochi-pane-edge-width` sets the width) and `RepanelStack` casts no shadow by default (`--mochi-stack-shadow`, `--mochi-stack-shadow-front`). The base pane in a stack is full-bleed: square, no edge, no shadow. The grabber draws its stroke at the pane's edge width.
- **Signature corners.** Floating surfaces take the waterdrop (`--mochi-floating-radius`, default `16px 4px 16px 16px`): `Dialog`, `PopupPanel`, the `Dropdown` menu and `FloatingPanel` (new `fullBleed` prop for square, no shadow). Phone sheets and `SlidingMenu` round the side facing the page (`--mochi-sheet-radius`). `PopupPanel` corner nubbins take the radius of the corner they sit on. New `waterdropRadius(size)` and the SCSS `waterdrop($size)` scale the drop with size, and the remaining off-ladder radii moved onto the corner ladder (new `--mochi-corner-xs: 4px`).
- **Nubbins, divider caps and the grabber drawn in code; SVG `Spinner`.** From the fix branch: no bitmap chrome, so it follows a radius or a stroke width. The `Spinner` is drawn as SVG from the traced GIFs (so `SpinnerProps` now extends SVG attributes). `scripts/nubbin-diff` and `scripts/spinner-diff` are the pixel-diff harnesses, with new cases for the 1px grabber and the waterdrop corner caps. `npm run spinner-diff` runs the latter.
- **Type ladder.** New `Label` (Mochi Bold 12/16, uppercase, on the 32px edge, `role="heading"`) and `Header size="display"` (Mochi Light 72/64, uppercase). `Table` column headers and `SlidingMenu` group labels take the label style.
- **Colour jobs, floors and greyscale cues.** One `--mochi-focus-ring` for every focused control; `--mochi-panel-text-muted` and panel-scoped button ink that pass 4.5:1 on dark panels; GridList captions meet 7:1 over imagery; warning `Badge`s use dark ink; selected `Item`, `Dropdown` option and `Pagination` page carry a cue that reads in greyscale (weight, a check, an accent bar). `SlidingMenu` drops its foreign palette for the `mochi-*` aliases. No bare hex colours remain in components.
- **`npm run check:tokens`.** A guard that fails on a bare hex in `src`, warns when a `var()` fallback disagrees with the light theme, and checks the guide's contrast floors (4.5:1 text, 7:1 over translucent grounds, 3:1 focus rings and control borders) and that the spinner's base fills equal the `--mochi-spinner-*` constants.
- **Motion: Full, Reduced, Off.** Motion tokens (`--mochi-ease-forward`, `--mochi-duration-stack`, `--mochi-ease-arrive`, `--mochi-duration-arrive`, `--mochi-duration-reduced`) replace the literals, and the phone sheet no longer exits slower than it arrives. `data-motion="full|reduced|off"` on `<html>` (as Pyxis sets it) or on a `ThemeWrapper`, else `prefers-reduced-motion`. Reduced is an opacity fade of at most 150ms with no travel, scale or stagger; Off is instant; drags always track exactly. The `Spinner` crossfades in 150ms and shows a still mark under Off.
- **Accessibility modes.** `ThemeWrapper` gains `motion`, `contrast` (`'system' | 'standard' | 'more'`) and `transparency` (`'system' | 'standard' | 'reduced'`). High contrast (`prefers-contrast: more` or `data-mochi-contrast="more"`) takes ink to pure black or white and surfaces solid; reduced transparency (`prefers-reduced-transparency` or `data-mochi-transparency="reduced"`) makes the scrim and `--mochi-surface-material` solid. High contrast makes scrims solid too.
- **Portaled surfaces use the wrapper's font.** `Dialog`, `PopupPanel` and the `Dropdown` menu render outside the `ThemeWrapper`, so they read `--mochi-font-body` (Prelude by default; `fontFamily` flows to it via `<html>`) instead of the host page's body font.
- **Ground.** `ThemeWrapper` `ground="vignette"` shades the edges of the area it covers with `--mochi-vignette` (an inset shadow, 30% black in light and 50% in dark). The default, `plain`, changes nothing.
- **Pointed motion.** `PopupPanel` grows from its nubbin tip; `ViewSelectButton`'s bar travels between segments and `onSelect` gets a third argument, `{ direction: 'forward' | 'back' | null, from, to }`.
- **Peeks and width classes.** `RepanelStack` `upcoming` / `onUpcomingOpen` show the predicted next panel as a right sliver (`--mochi-peek-width`, 80px; `--mochi-peek-width-narrow`, 20px), `keepPast` (default `true`) keeps a left sliver of the panel you came from, and `Repanel` takes `hint`, `sequence` and `peekLabel` for one clue while peeking. Slivers are named buttons at least 44px tall. A peek is a door, not a second panel: the right peek shows only its corner, its cropped Header and one clue, the left sliver only its corner and the start of its Header, and on a narrow stack both are only the curve. The slivers overlap the front panel the Mochi way: the right peek lies over its right edge by the 45px scallop and the front panel's curve lies over the left sliver, each showing the surface beneath, while the front panel's content is padded clear of the peek. The grabber answers Enter and Space. New `SCREEN_NARROW` (768), `SCREEN_WIDE` (1280) and `useWidthClass(ref)`; `RepanelStack` decides narrow from its own width, and `--mochi-screen-narrow` / `--mochi-screen-wide` mirror the constants.
- **`forwardRef` and fixes.** `Button`, `Dropdown` and `Toggle` forward their ref to the root element. `PopupPanel` actions now pass `type` to `Button` (it passed `$type`, which was never read, so every action was `normal`). The unused `src/Button/Button.jsx` stub is deprecated and goes in 0.7.
- **Updated TypeScript declarations.** `Label`, `Header.size`, `ThemeWrapper` `motion` / `contrast` / `transparency` and a doc comment on the main 0.6.0 tokens, `FloatingPanel.fullBleed`, `RepanelStack` `upcoming` / `onUpcomingOpen` / `keepPast`, `Repanel` `hint` / `sequence` / `peekLabel`, `ViewSelectButton` `onSelect` detail, ref types, `useWidthClass`, `SCREEN_*` and `waterdropRadius`.

## Upgrading from 0.5.x

| Change | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Panes draw a 1px left-only edge (it was 2px on the top and left in #666), and `RepanelStack` casts no shadow. The base pane is square with no edge. | To restore the old look: `--mochi-pane-edge-width: 2px; --mochi-pane-edge: #666;` and `--mochi-stack-shadow: drop-shadow(-6px 0 14px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14)); --mochi-stack-shadow-front: drop-shadow(-10px 0 26px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22));`. |
| The `Dropdown` menu, `PopupPanel`, `FloatingPanel` and sheets take waterdrop corners (24px uniform before). | Set `--mochi-floating-radius` (and `--mochi-sheet-radius` for sheets and `SlidingMenu`), or pass `fullBleed` to `FloatingPanel` for square. |
| Light `--color-primary` is darker, `#357099` (text now 5.3:1 on white), and the light focus ring is the primary rather than the accent blue. | Override `--color-primary` / `--mochi-focus-ring` through `tokens` to restore. |
| Selected `Item` is no longer outlined: it gets an accent tint and bold title. The `Dropdown` selected option gets a check, and the `Pagination` current page is ink with an accent bar instead of white on blue. GridList selection uses `--mochi-accent`. | None, unless you styled these selectors. |
| `Badge` `variant="warning"` uses dark ink (`#404040`) instead of white on amber. | Pass `color` to choose the ink. |
| `RepanelStack` keeps a left sliver of the panel you came from when collapsed (`keepPast` defaults to `true`). | Pass `keepPast={false}` for the old collapse to 0 width. |
| Reduced motion now fades (opacity, at most 150ms) instead of switching animation off, and `data-motion` is read from `<html>` as well as a `ThemeWrapper`. | Use `motion="off"` (or `data-motion="off"`) for instant. |
| Copy changed: "Loading…" (a real ellipsis), "Select an option…", and one wording for the `DateInput` placeholder ("Select date…"). | Update any text matching on the old strings. |
| The PNG and GIF sprites are removed from `src` (nubbins, divider caps and the spinner are drawn in code), and `Spinner` renders an `<svg>`, not an `<img>`. | Nothing, unless you deep-imported the images or styled the spinner as an `img`. |
| `Table` column headers use the label style (Mochi Bold 12, uppercase), and `SlidingMenu` group labels are Mochi Bold. The `SlidingMenu` rounds the side facing the page, and its active row, success and error colours come from the `mochi-*` tokens. | Override the tokens, or the `.mochi-table-header` styles. |
| `Header` has `size="display"` (72/64, uppercase); `Header` tracking stays `normal`. | Optional. |
| `npm run check:tokens` is new. | Run it in CI to keep bare hex colours out of `src`. |
